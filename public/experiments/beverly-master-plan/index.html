Checking Beverly's master plan, one area at a time

We've spent a lot of time on 37 Enon St, and there's plenty to weigh at the project level. But the letter read at the October 27 Planning Board hearing, from a member of the master plan committee, pointed at something bigger. The plan imagined North Beverly as a walkable village someday, and the zoning and street changes to get there never happened before a project arrived. That's less about this one project than about a chance the neighborhood may be missing.

I hadn't spent much time with the plan until now, so I read it. Then, as an experiment, I got curious about the city's scorecard against it since 2021. Is North Beverly the exception or the pattern? This prototype tries three areas, lining up what the plan asked for against the projects and zoning changes since.
